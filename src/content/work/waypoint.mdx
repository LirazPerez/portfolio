## Process, including what didn't work

<Intro
  headline="I ran two quick moderated sessions, one participant each, and changed the product after each one."
  lede={'The first mobile home tested as "too much like a spreadsheet", my "Re-check" label was misread, and I learned to stop explaining terms mid-task because it weakened the findings.'}
/>

<CompareTable
  caption="What I saw in testing, and what changed"
  columns={['What I saw in testing', 'What changed']}
  rows={[
    ['Both went to Documents, not the record', 'Shortcuts to the record, and every record linked to its source'],
    ['"Re-check" read as "renew the prescription"', '"Check medications are still correct", with the medication names'],
    ['The chat went unnoticed', 'Intro message and suggested questions, and Ask always open on the desktop home'],
    ['"Looks too much like a spreadsheet"', 'The home reworked in three rounds, then the desktop workspace'],
    ['Offline access raised as essential', '"Save for offline" in the pre-trip checklist'],
  ]}
