## Key decisions

<Intro headline="Three decisions carry the whole idea." />

<Decision
  n={1}
  title="Check availability before a request becomes a purchase."
  summary="The system looks across every active project and offers a transfer in the same form."
  considered="Considered: a low-stock alert to procurement."
  verdict="Rejected, because the same shortage would enter twice, once from the field request and once from the alert, and procurement would buy instead of transfer."
>
  <Screen
    project="aeropulse"
    name="decision-1-transfer"

The shortage is met by a transfer from another project instead of a new purchase."
  />
</Decision>

<Decision
  n={2}
  title="Match the alert to how urgent it actually is."
  summary="Recoverable stays in the row. Urgent and unexplained goes straight to the evidence."
  considered="Considered: one alert style for everything."
  verdict="Rejected, because when everything is red, nothing is."
>
  <Screen
    project="aeropulse"
    name="decision-2-low-stock"

  />
  <Screen
    project="aeropulse"
    name="decision-2-defects"
    alt="Defect analysis, first the whole page with its defect chart and root causes, then a zoom to the top: a red banner says Wear and Tear accounts for 63 percent of defects, a concentrated root cause to investigate, above the totals: 8 defects, 4 open or in repair, 2 with high readiness impact."
  />
</Decision>

<Decision
  n={3}
  title="Confirm with evidence, not with a status."
  summary="A shipment is marked arrived when someone attaches the photo and the bill of lading."
  considered="Trade-off: more clicks on site."
  verdict="Worth it, because a status nobody confirmed is the number nobody trusts."
>
  <Screen
    project="aeropulse"

  />
</Decision>
